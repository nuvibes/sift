Music is where you set up how Sift recognizes the song in a file by its sound. To open it, go to [Settings > Music](/settings/music).

Come here to add your AcoustID key so Sift can name songs, or to choose when music fingerprints are made. Sift makes each fingerprint with Chromaprint on your computer and looks the song up on AcoustID.

![The Music pane in Settings](../../../assets/screens/settings-music.jpg)

## On this pane

The first rows have no heading: naming songs with AcoustID, and your key. Then the pane draws one group:

- <a id="music.fingerprints"></a>**Music fingerprints**: a short summary of a file's sound, made on your computer, so Sift can match files that share a song. It shows when making them runs, and **More settings**.

## Rows the pane draws by hand

- **AcoustID key**: your AcoustID application key. **Save** encrypts it, and Sift never shows it again.
- **More settings**: **Edit** opens which connection Sift uses to reach AcoustID.

A lookup sends a fingerprint of the file's sound and its length to AcoustID, never the file.
